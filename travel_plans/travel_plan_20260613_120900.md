**Day 3: Jaipur**

* Take a morning flight from New Delhi to Jaipur (approximately 1 hour)
* Check-in to a hotel in Jaipur, such as the Aman or a similar luxury hotel
* Visit the Amber Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal
* Explore the local markets and try some street food

**Day 4: Jaipur**

* Visit the Nahargarh Fort and enjoy the views of the city
* Explore the Jal Mahal and take a stroll around the Man Sagar Lake
* Visit the Birla Mandir and enjoy a traditional Rajasthani dinner

**Day 5: Varanasi**

* Take a morning flight from Jaipur to Varanasi (approximately 1.5 hours)
* Check-in to a hotel in Varanasi, such as a business hotel or a hostel
* Visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple and take a stroll along the Ganges River
* Explore the local markets and try some street food

**Day 6: Varanasi**

* Take a sunrise boat ride on the Ganges River
* Visit the Banaras Hindu University and the Bharat Mata Temple
* Explore the local markets and try some street food

**Day 7: Goa**

* Take a morning flight from Varanasi to Goa (approximately 2.5 hours)
* Check-in to a hotel in Goa, such as a beach resort or a hostel
* Spend the day relaxing on the beach or exploring the local markets

**Day 8: Goa**

* Visit the Fort Aguada and the Chapora Fort
* Take a stroll around the Panjim city and visit the Basilica of Bom Jesus
* Enjoy a traditional Goan dinner and relax on the beach

**Day 9: Goa**

* Take a day trip to the Dudhsagar Waterfall (approximately 1 hour drive)
* Visit the spice plantations and take a stroll around the jungle
* Return to the hotel in the evening and enjoy a relaxing dinner

**Day 10: Departure**

* Spend the morning relaxing on the beach or exploring the local markets
* Depart for the airport and take a flight back to Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) and then connect to Auckland International
